Startrails

Privacy policy · 13 November 2026

Startrails does not collect any information about who you are. The only data that leaves your phone is described under "Game updates" below.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings and the signs you draw are saved on your iPhone so the game remembers them. They never leave the device, except as part of your own iCloud or device backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Game updates

When the game starts with an internet connection, it asks Expo (the service that delivers small game updates) whether a new version is ready. That request carries a random number made on your phone for this copy of the game, the game's version, and, if the game failed to start last time, a short error message so a broken update can be rolled back. Like any internet request, it also reveals your IP address. Expo keeps these records to run the update service. They are not linked to your name or Apple ID, are not used for advertising, and are not shared with data brokers. See Expo's description for details.

What we don't do

No accounts, no ads, nothing recorded about how you play, no tracking across apps or websites. The game plays fully offline; without a connection it simply skips the update check.

Children

The game asks no one for personal information, children included. The update check above works the same for every player.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is.
