Voyage between constellations

Fly a small ship along a line of light and draw the night sky, star by star. Find the twelve signs of the zodiac and read the myths and the astronomy behind each one.

Support

Found a bug, lost your progress, or have a question? Write to Jakobcv@me.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you're using.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone. It is kept when the game updates, and it comes back if you restore your iPhone from an iCloud or device backup. Deleting the game deletes its progress.
