About the game

Somewhere far from Earth, a lone pilgrim carries old star charts and a list of names no one has said aloud in a thousand years. The stars are all still there, but the shapes are gone: they were only ever seen from one small world. So the pilgrim flies, retracing them one by one, and leaves memories along the way for you to find.

Each flight carries you farther out. Steer well and your trail finds the constellations: all twelve signs of the zodiac, then Orion, the Great Bear, the Southern Cross and more, each plotted from real star positions. Every one you find opens its stories: the myths people told about it, and what astronomers have since learned about its stars. Turn a constellation in 3D and you see how far away each of its stars really is, and how they only line up from where we stand.

Features

  • Twenty constellations to find: the twelve signs of the zodiac and eight famous others
  • Old myths and real astronomy for each: double stars, meteor showers, exploded stars and black holes
  • Turn any constellation you've found in 3D and see how far away each of its stars really is
  • A quiet story, told in memories found along the way, with no cutscenes
  • Calm flights for drifting (no way to fail), Standard for the open sky, and Hard for no second chances
  • Draw your own constellations once you find a blank chart
  • Short runs, easy to pick up; plays fully offline
